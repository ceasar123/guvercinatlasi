Sivas Tumbler

Breed Atlas

The Sivas tumbler (Sivas taklacısı) is a tumbler said to be kept by thousands of people in the city whose name it bears. News stories have been written about it, but its trace in scientific publications is limited.

History: a single row in a table

In the peer-reviewed publications examined, the breed appears most clearly in a table: a review published in 2025, listing the native pigeon breeds of Türkiye, gives the name "Sivas Taklacısı" its own row and lists its region as Sivas. The same table also lists the Mardin, Urfa, Kayseri, and Konya tumblers in their own rows [1]. According to the review, there are about 800 pigeon breeds in the world, and about 100 of them are raised in Türkiye [1].

An older compilation, from 2012, describes a breed called "Takla" and names Sivas, along with Mardin and Şanlıurfa, among the provinces where this breed is mostly raised (citing Gürsu, 2010) [2]. The names and flight of Takla are described on the Mardin Clap Tumbler (Takla) page; for a description that sets the Sivas tumbler's tumbling apart from other tumblers, see the Open file section.

One confusion should be prevented at the outset: according to the same compilation, a separate breed of singing type called Kumru is also raised in Sivas and the surrounding provinces [2]. This page describes the tumbler, not Kumru.

There are signs that pigeon keeping in the city is still lively today. According to a news report from April 2025, a tumbler pigeon festival, described in the report as "bu yıl ilk kez gerçekleştirilen" ["held for the first time this year"], took place in Sivas; according to the chairman of the festival committee, about 3,000 people in the province keep pigeons [8]. In 2021, a researcher had relayed that the breeders' association had identified nearly 6,000 breeders [4]. Both figures are statements with unexplained methods, and they differ greatly from each other.

How to recognize it

No published measurement or written breed standard that could be consulted to recognize the Sivas tumbler was found. The description we have rests on a statement made to the press in 2021 by Taner Daştan, of the university team studying the breed [4]. These are researcher statements; they are not an article supported by numbers.

  • Size: According to Taner Daştan of the team, the Sivas pigeon is, in build, slightly larger than the Mardin pigeon and slightly smaller than the Urfa pigeon [4].
  • Color: According to Daştan, nearly 11 colors have been identified in the birds; dark colors are encountered more often [4].
  • Wing: The researcher had said that the wing tips show a reddish tinge they had not seen in other pigeons, and that the team would explain after laboratory work whether this has a genetic basis [4].
  • Flight: According to the researcher's statement, in fieldwork the birds can stay in the air for 8 to 10 hours once they start flying; some breeders said they had flown them for 14 hours. The researchers attribute this to lung capacity [4]. This is an attempted explanation, not a measured finding.

The characters the team examined are, however, on record: many characters such as body and wing length, wingspan, leg length, head structure, beak length and depth, chest circumference, and chest width [4]. In February 2021, the association chairman said that 180 nests had been visited and nearly 600 birds examined [6]. In August 2022, the team announced that the morphological study was finished, that they had also researched flight performance and vocal characteristics, and that genetic analysis was continuing with feather samples [7].

Registration: what is said versus what is documented

The common subject of the news reports we found on the Sivas tumbler is registration. In 2019, academics who are members of the Sivas Pigeons Research, Development and Conservation Association (Sivas Güvercinleri Araştırma Geliştirme ve Koruma Derneği) started work to register the "taklacı ve oyuncu" ["tumbler and performer"] pigeon breed. The team included Sevgi Durna Daştan, Yusuf Ziya Oğrak, and Taner Daştan of Sivas Cumhuriyet University and Faruk Balcı of Uludağ University [5].

The statements do not agree with each other over time. In a 2019 university announcement, the team says an application was made to the Ministry of Agriculture and Forestry's Breed Registration Commission [5]. In February 2021, the association chairman says that, by the chairman's estimate, the registration procedures would be carried out at the end of that year [6]. In August 2022, however, the team says the application will be made after the experiments are finished; the same statement also says that "bugüne kadar" ["until now"] no morphological or genetic study had been carried out on the breed and that the breed had not been registered [7].

An article published in June 2026 writes that six of the regional native breeds have been officially registered and lists them: Bursa, Hünkâri, Edremit Kelebek, Alabadem, Trakya, and Muğla [3]. The Sivas tumbler is not on this list.

Savaş and Erdem add a general warning about such processes: in their view, pigeon fanciers attach more importance to the name of their locality coming to the fore than to the biological and historical facts about the breed. The authors argue that defining a breed requires biological, cultural, and historical data together [9].

Open file

What the sources say with certainty about this breed, and questions not yet studied.

Known

  • In Table 1 of a 2025 review, "Sivas Taklacı" is on a separate row, with its region given as Sivas [1].
  • A 2012 compilation names Sivas among the provinces where the "Takla" breed is mostly raised (citing Gürsu, 2010) [2].
  • Academics who are members of the Sivas Pigeons Research, Development and Conservation Association started work in 2019 to register the "taklacı ve oyuncu" ["tumbler and performer"] pigeon breed [5].
  • In August 2022 the team announced that the morphological study was finished and that genetic analysis was continuing with feather samples [7].
  • The Sivas tumbler is not among the six registered regional breeds listed by an article published in June 2026 [3].

Not yet studied

  • Breed standard and measurements: No published measurement for size, color, or wing was found, nor a written description that sets the tumbling apart from other tumblers; the existing description rests on researcher statements [4].
  • Genetic and measurement results: We could not find a record showing that the team's morphological and genetic studies were published in a peer-reviewed journal; an explanation of the genetic basis of the reddish wing tips has not yet been seen either [4] [7].
  • Registration: The wording about an application in the 2019 announcement and the 2022 statement do not agree; we could not find a registration in the Official Gazette (Resmî Gazete) or in Ministry records [5] [7].
  • Origin: A document describing the breed's origin cannot yet be shown.
  • Number of breeders: The estimates of 6,000 and 3,000 for the number of breeders in Sivas stand as statements with unexplained methods [4] [8].
