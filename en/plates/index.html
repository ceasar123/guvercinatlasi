Engraved plates

Engraved plate posters of Turkish pigeon breeds. One plate for each of the twelve breeds in the Breed Atlas.

The engraved plates are drawings made with AI, not photographs. For the breed standard, rely on written sources.

Each plate is available in our Etsy shop as a high-resolution printable file: five frame ratios, 300 dpi, instant download. The sourced account of each breed is on its breed page.
